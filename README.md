# رفيق الحفظ — Rafeeq al-Hifz

تطبيق مجاني لحفظ القرآن الكريم ومراجعته، وقفٌ لوجه الله بلا إعلانات ولا اشتراكات.

A free, ad-free Quran memorization web app (sadaqah jariyah):

- **Daily wird**: review what's due first, then a new portion sized to your daily goal
- **Guided memorization in 4 steps**: listen (with repetition) → repeat while reading → recite with first-word hints → recite from memory
- **Voice checking**: hidden words appear as you recite them correctly (browser Arabic speech recognition)
- **Spaced-repetition reviews**, chained one after another, with a suggested rating from your mistakes
- **Progress**: 30-juz map, consistency heatmap, retention strength, export/import backup
- Full Uthmani Mushaf (114 surahs, 6,236 ayat); six reciters; light/dark themes
- **Installable mobile app (PWA)**: add to home screen, runs full-screen and works offline

Open https://drbinyamin.github.io/rafeeq-alhifz/ on your phone → Share → *Add to Home Screen* (iPhone) or ⋮ → *Install app* (Android).
